“CLOCK IN the world with KOI” Global Magnet Badge Collection Challenge

"Clock in" isn't just the start of a daily routine — it's also a way to mark a memorable journey.
From clocking in at work, to collecting footprints around the world, these two meanings quietly meet at KOI.

Today, KOI Group has expanded to 17 countries and over 700 stores worldwide.
Wherever you travel, we hope you’ll come across KOI on your journey, enjoy the familiar taste of home, and feel the warmth of an old friend by your side.

To celebrate KOI Group’s 20th Anniversary in 2026, we are launching the Global Limited Magnet Badge Collection Challenge!
Each participating country features a unique cultural magnet badge design. Travel with your magnet badges, take photos, clock-in, and collect them to complete your KOI world travel map — and stand a chance to win USD 2,000!
 

Campaign Period: 2026/1/1 – 2026/12/31

Participating Countries:
All KOI stores located in Singapore, Indonesia, Cambodia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Bangladesh, Laos, Brunei, China, Japan, the United Arab Emirates, and France, together with KOI Group sub-brand FIFTYLAN stores in the United States, Canada and Australia.
 

⭐ Level 1 — Tea Specialist
Collect 3 different country magnet badges to unlock the Tea Specialist level.
⚠️For every 3 magnet badges collected, you may redeem 1 drink at any store under KOI Group worldwide with the drink card included in the packaging (no size or topping restrictions). Drink cards must be surrendered to the store after redemption.
 

⭐⭐ Level 2 — Global Tea Artisan
Collect 8 different country magnet badges and be among the first 20 participants to receive 50 KOI Group drink vouchers.
⚠️Redemption requirement:
For each country collected, you must provide a photo of yourself holding the country’s magnet badge in front of a KOI Group store in that respective country. Both the store and magnet badge must be clearly identifiable.
 

⭐⭐⭐ Level 3 — Golden Flavor Grandmaster
Collect 12 or more different country magnet badges and be among the first 5 participants to win USD 2,000 in cash.
⚠️Redemption requirement:
For each country collected, you must provide a photo of yourself holding the country’s magnet badge in front of a KOI Group store in that respective country. Both the store and magnet badge must be clearly identifiable.
